# Third-party asset credits

This inventory records assets currently used by Evanopolis V1 and the source
and license details needed for a future in-game credits screen. It lists the
selected files used by the game, rather than every audition file in the asset
directories.

## Music

- **Sketchbook 2025-11-26** (`Sketchbook 2025-11-26.ogg`) by **Abstraction**
  from the [Music Loop Bundle](https://tallbeard.itch.io/music-loop-bundle),
  published by Tallbeard Studios. License: **CC0 1.0**. Credit is optional;
  the creator requests the name “Abstraction” and a link to the pack if credit
  is given. The downloaded pack's [license](../godot/assets/music-loop-bundle-2025-q4/_LICENSE.txt)
  is retained in the repo. Used as the looping game background music.

## Sound effects

- **Casino Audio** by **Kenney**:
  [source](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio), **CC0 1.0**.
  The downloaded pack's [license](../godot/assets/sfx/kenney-casino-audio/License.txt)
  is retained in the repo. Used files: `dice-throw-1.ogg` for dice rolls and
  `card-place-2.ogg` when a pending card first appears.
- **Interface Sounds** by **Kenney**:
  [source](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), **CC0 1.0**.
  Used files: `bong_001.ogg` for main action buttons and `glass_006.ogg` when
  a panel opens.
- **Universal UI/Menu Soundpack** by **Nathan Gibson** (Cyrex Studios):
  [source](https://cyrex-studios.itch.io/universal-ui-soundpack),
  [**CC BY 4.0**](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Credit Nathan Gibson and link the pack in the in-game credits. The downloaded
  pack's [readme](../godot/assets/sfx/UI%20Soundpack/readme.txt) is retained in
  the repo. Used files: `Minimalist7.wav` for pawn steps, `African4.ogg` for
  positive card effects, and `Retro11.ogg` for negative card effects. Source
  audio files are unchanged; playback volume is adjusted in the game.
