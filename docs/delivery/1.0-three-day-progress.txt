EVANOPOLIS 1.0 — 3-DAY DELIVERY PROGRESS

Completed
- Merged PRs #15, #16, and #17.
- Enabled automatic deployment to the official AWS environment on every push to the main branch.
- Successfully tested free rooms with six players.

- Added proactive client resynchronization when returning to a browser tab and every five seconds.
- Updated free-game invitation pages to show live room occupancy and the player's actual next available seat.

- Added authentication and room-capacity checks to prevent payment attempts after every seat has already been purchased.
- Added more robust automatic payment confirmation checks, aligning paid-game UX with the three-confirmation rule and reducing manual verification after payment.
- Added 24-hour paid-room expiration and atomic pre-payment seat reservations through the payment service.
- Bound payment verification and recovery to the reservation id, preventing the wallet transaction from opening when capacity is unavailable.
- Added paid-client wallet reauthentication after JWT expiry so the same wallet can recover its seat without paying again.
- Accepted the final-seat flow on the official AWS deployment with three wallets: the last seat was visible as reserved before payment, the third wallet was rejected before allowance approval, payment changed the seat to paid, and reverse connection order preserved Player 1 and Player 2.
- Accepted paid-seat recovery after refresh and complete browser closure: the same wallet re-entered without another payment as Player 2 with balance, position, properties, active turn, and subsequent turn handoff preserved.
